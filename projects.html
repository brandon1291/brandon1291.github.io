Southside Boxing Gym (sample)

A sample website for a fictional local boxing gym. I built it to practice the goal I'd have for any real client: help people searching for classes find the gym and take the next step.

The goal

Get beginners in the area to find the gym on Google and book a free first class.

What I did

  • Wrote a page title and description around what people search for, like boxing classes for beginners in Birmingham.
  • Made a mobile-first layout, since most people look up gyms on their phones. The schedule scrolls sideways on small screens instead of breaking the page.
  • Put a clear call to action at the top and again at the bottom: book your free class.
  • Added local business structured data (schema markup) so search engines can read the gym's address and hours.
  • Listed classes, schedule, and pricing on one page so visitors can decide quickly.
