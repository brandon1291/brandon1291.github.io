5 quick fixes to help your small business website get found on Google

Published November 18, 2026 by Brandon

You don't need a big budget to improve how your website shows up in search. These five fixes are simple, and many businesses haven't done them.

1. Give every page a clear title and description

The page title is the blue link people see in Google, so it should say what you do and where. "Southside Boxing Gym | Beginner Classes in Birmingham, AL" tells people far more than "Home". Add a short meta description too, one or two sentences that make someone want to click.

2. Make sure your site works well on a phone

Many people look up local businesses on their phones, and Google looks at the mobile version of your site. Open your site on your own phone. If you have to zoom to read text, tap tiny links, or scroll sideways, fix that first.

3. Make your pages load fast

Slow pages make visitors leave. The most common cause is large images. Resize photos to the size they actually appear, compress them, and remove extra scripts or plugins you don't need. You can test your site for free with Google's PageSpeed Insights.

4. Make the next step obvious

Getting visitors is only half the job. Every page should make it clear what to do next, such as "Call now", "Book your free class", or "Get a quote". Use specific wording, put the button near the top, and repeat it further down the page.

5. Set up your Google Business Profile

If customers come to a location or you serve a local area, a free Google Business Profile is one of the best things you can do. Add your name, address, phone number, hours, and photos, and keep them identical to what's on your website. Asking happy customers for reviews helps too.
